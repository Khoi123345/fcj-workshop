During my internship at **First Cloud AI Journey (FCAJ)** and the development of the **AI Dungeon RPG Adventure Game** from **June 22, 2026** to **August 15, 2026**, I had the incredible opportunity to learn, explore, and apply cutting-edge technologies to a real-world product. I was responsible for designing and implementing a 100% Serverless architecture on AWS (using Lambda, DynamoDB, Cognito) and building the game client in Unity 2D. Through this project, I significantly improved my skills in **C# programming (.NET 8 and Unity), Cloud-native system design**, and gained deep practical experience in integrating **Generative AI (AWS Bedrock)** to create dynamic storytelling in games.

### Areas for Improvement

Looking back at the entire process, I recognize a few areas where I need to improve to better myself:

* **Enhancing logical thinking and communication skills:** Sometimes I have many ideas, but I struggle to express them clearly. I need to organize my thoughts more logically so that I can present them in an easy-to-understand manner, helping peers and colleagues grasp the concepts quickly.
* **Expanding knowledge for better problem-solving:** My ability to identify and resolve issues is occasionally limited by a lack of practical experience. I need to proactively research and learn new solutions to broaden my perspective when facing challenges.
* **Effective time management:** My time allocation between learning and working on the project has not always been optimal. I need to plan my schedule more scientifically and minimize unproductive free time to boost my overall efficiency.
* **Developing interpersonal skills and relationship building:** I realize that a positive work environment drives team performance. Therefore, I need to be more open in my communication, actively building strong relationships with those around me to foster a cohesive and productive working atmosphere.
